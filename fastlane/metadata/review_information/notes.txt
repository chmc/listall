ListAll is a privacy-focused list management app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch with iCloud sync. No login or demo account is needed.

KEY TESTING INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Create a new list by tapping the + button on the main screen, or start from a template
2. Add items to the list with title, description, and quantity
3. Add photos to items using the camera or photo library
4. Type similar item names multiple times to see smart suggestions appear
5. Cross out items by tapping the checkbox
6. Archive a list from its context menu (long-press)
7. Enable Face ID/Touch ID lock in Settings (if available on the test device)

WATCHOS TESTING:
1. Open the watchOS app on the paired Apple Watch
2. Lists sync automatically from iPhone
3. Tap items to mark them complete; changes sync back to iPhone

PRIVACY PERMISSIONS:
- Camera: attach photos to list items
- Photo Library: select existing photos for list items
- Face ID/Touch ID: optional app lock
- iCloud: syncs data across the user's devices when signed in to iCloud

NO THIRD-PARTY SERVICES:
- All data is stored locally or in the user's iCloud
- No analytics, tracking, ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases
- The app works completely offline
